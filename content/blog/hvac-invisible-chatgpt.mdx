# Why Your HVAC Business Is Invisible to Google AND ChatGPT (And How to Fix Both)

Try this right now.

Open Google and search "AC repair Columbus OH."

Then open ChatGPT and ask "Who should I call for AC repair in Columbus?"

Compare the results.

The businesses at the top of Google? Not the same as the ones ChatGPT recommends. And if you're not on either list, you're invisible to two completely different audiences of homeowners.

## Two Different Games. One Business to Run.

In 2024, homeowners relied on Google. You needed to rank on page 1.

In 2026, homeowners use both. Some go to Google. Some ask ChatGPT or Perplexity. A growing number use Google's AI Overviews and never click a single link.

Here's the problem: the strategies that get you ranked on Google don't automatically get you recommended by AI engines. They look at different signals. They reward different things.

Most HVAC contractors are losing on both fronts.

## Why Google Rankings Are Harder Than Ever

Google's 2025 Helpful Content update penalized thin, templated HVAC pages. The contractors winning on Google now have:

- Deep, specific content about their local market
- Real technician expertise visible on the page
- Strong Google reviews with keyword-rich responses
- Consistent NAP (name, address, phone) across every directory

Most contractors have none of these. They have a five-page website, 12 Google reviews, and a contact form.

## Why AI Search Is a Completely Different Problem

When someone asks ChatGPT "best HVAC company in Columbus," it doesn't check Google's index. It answers based on what it's been trained on and what it can verify across the web.

AI engines recommend businesses that:

**Answer specific questions in detail.** "What does a heat pump installation cost in Columbus?" — not "We install heat pumps." The difference matters.

**Have expertise signals across the web.** Reviews, mentions in local publications, citations from industry sources. AI systems cross-reference everything.

**Publish consistently.** A blog post from 2019 doesn't count. Weekly content tells AI engines this business is active and authoritative.

We scanned 5 HVAC contractors in Columbus across Google, ChatGPT, and Perplexity.

One contractor — Columbus Worthington Air — appeared 7 times across AI engines. Their Google Maps listing sits in the top 3 for 14 local search terms.

The other four contractors had a combined total of zero AI citations and ranked beyond page 2 on Google for most service searches.

## The Fix Covers Both

Getting visible on Google AND AI search requires the same foundation: genuine expertise, consistent content, and strong reviews. But the execution is different.

For Google:
- Location-specific service pages with real pricing data
- Google Business Profile optimization
- Review velocity (more reviews, responded to quickly)
- Internal linking and page structure

For AI search:
- Long-form content that directly answers questions
- Author attribution with real credentials
- Citations from third-party sources
- Structured data (schema markup)

The good news: most of the work overlaps. A well-written blog post targeting "AC repair cost Columbus 2026" can rank on Google AND get cited by ChatGPT. You don't need two separate strategies — you need one strategy that covers both.

## What This Means for Your Business

Every week you're not publishing content and building reviews, your competitors are getting further ahead on both Google and AI search.

The gap compounds. A contractor who starts this process today will be significantly harder to catch in 12 months.

The ones who move first own their market.
