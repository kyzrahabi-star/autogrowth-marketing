# Why Your HVAC Business Is Invisible to ChatGPT (And How to Fix It)

Ask ChatGPT "who should I call for AC repair in Columbus OH" right now.

Go ahead. I'll wait.

What came back? A short list of businesses — maybe three or four. If your business isn't on that list, someone else got that call.

## Why This Is Happening Now

AI search engines — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews — don't search the web the way Google did in 2015. They don't scan blue links and pick the top result. They read content, find businesses that answer questions clearly and specifically, and recommend those businesses by name.

The businesses that get recommended? They're writing content that answers real questions. "What does AC repair cost in Columbus in July?" "How long does a furnace installation take?" "Is a heat pump worth it in central Ohio?"

Most HVAC contractors aren't writing that content. They have a five-page website with "We're the best HVAC company in Columbus" and a contact form. That doesn't get you cited.

## What "AI Visibility" Actually Means

When we scan AI search engines for an HVAC contractor, we're looking for how many times their business gets mentioned when someone asks relevant questions. We call this your AI citation count.

We scanned 5 HVAC contractors in Columbus earlier this year. One of them — Columbus Worthington Air — showed up 7 times across ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. The other four? A combined total of zero citations.

That's not a small gap. That's invisibility.

## The Fix Is Straightforward (But Takes Work)

AI search engines cite businesses that:

1. **Answer specific questions** about their service area. Not "we serve Columbus." But "here's what AC repair costs in Columbus in 2026, broken down by problem type."

2. **Have real expertise on the page.** Authored content. A real technician's name. Credentials. Experience. AI systems are trained to detect expertise signals.

3. **Publish consistently.** One blog post from 2019 won't cut it. Weekly content — even short posts — signals that this business is actively engaged.

4. **Get mentioned across the web.** Third-party citations matter. Reviews, local directories, industry associations. AI systems cross-reference sources.

## What This Means for Your Business

If you're running an HVAC business and you're not actively publishing content that AI systems can read and recommend — you're losing calls you don't know you're losing.

The homeowner who asked ChatGPT last Tuesday got a recommendation. It wasn't you.

That's the $50,000 question nobody in trades is asking yet.

The ones who ask it first — and fix it first — will own their market for the next decade.
